## Why AI-Assisted Coding Matters

AI-assisted coding is transforming how modern front-end developers build applications. By pairing human domain knowledge with AI copilots, developers can accelerate boilerplate creation, write comprehensive test suites, debug complex edge cases, and explore alternative architectural approaches with unprecedented speed.
