## Why Angular Matters

Angular is Google's flagship enterprise framework. Engineered with TypeScript from the ground up, Angular combines standalone components, fine-grained Signals reactivity, a two-tiered hierarchical Dependency Injection system, reactive forms, client-side routing, and hybrid Server-Side Rendering (SSR) into a unified platform.

## Curriculum Overview

This master curriculum spans 3 difficulty tiers across 42 comprehensive lessons:
1. **Level 1 — Beginner (Lessons 1–13)**: Angular Fundamentals, TypeScript foundations, Standalone Components, Templates & Data Binding, Modern Control Flow (`@if`, `@for`, `@switch`), Signals, Component Communication (`input()`, `output()`, `model()`), Services & DI (`inject()`), Routing, Forms, HTTP, Styling, and 7 Beginner Projects.
2. **Level 2 — Intermediate (Lessons 14–25)**: Advanced Components & Render Hooks (`afterNextRender`), Advanced Signals & `computed()`, RxJS & Stream Pipelines, Advanced Functional Guards & Resolvers, Advanced DI & `InjectionToken`, HTTP Interceptors, State Management (`@ngrx/signals` SignalStore), Typed Reactive Forms & `ControlValueAccessor`, Unit & E2E Testing (Vitest & Playwright), Angular CLI Schematics & Budgets, Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 & Angular CDK), and 8 Intermediate Projects.
3. **Level 3 — Advanced (Lessons 26–42)**: Angular Internals (Ivy, `LView`/`TView`), Change Detection & Zoneless Angular, Push-Pull Reactivity & Graph Theory, SSR & Non-Destructive Hydration with Event Replay, Performance Engineering & Core Web Vitals, Advanced `@defer`, Angular CDK Headless Primitives, Domain-Driven Large-Scale Architecture, Nx Monorepos, Micro-Frontends & Module Federation, Enterprise Security (OAuth 2.0 PKCE, CSP, Trusted Types), Production Observability & Sentry RUM, CI/CD Pipelines, Advanced Contract & Visual Regression Testing, Custom Angular Libraries (`ng-packagr`), Advanced TypeScript Meta-Programming, and 3 Senior Capstone Projects.
