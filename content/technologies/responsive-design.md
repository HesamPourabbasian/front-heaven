## Why responsive design matters

Most of the world browses the web on a phone — screens a fraction of the size of a laptop display. A site that only looks right on a desktop is, by definition, broken for most of its users. Responsive design is the discipline of making pages adapt: layouts reflow, navigation collapses, images rescale, and text stays readable at every size.

It is also where much of the professional craft of front-end development lives. The fluid toolkit (`minmax()` grids, `clamp()`, `dvh`), media queries and content-driven breakpoints, container queries, and the touch-first interaction layer are the skills that separate sites that merely fit on a phone from products that feel native on one.

## What you will learn

- The responsive mindset and the universal patterns every site follows
- Fluid layouts: flexible units, self-reflowing grids, `clamp()` typography, `dvh`
- Media queries: `min-width` mobile-first syntax, ranges, `prefers-*` queries
- Mobile-first development and content-driven breakpoints
- Responsive components: navigation, card grids, tables, forms, images
- Modern container queries and `cq` units
- Touch targets, hover-free interaction and motion preferences
- Testing and debugging across devices with DevTools

By the end of this stage you will be able to build a complete, mobile-first, professionally responsive page — and verify it works at every width from a 320px phone to a 1440px desktop.
