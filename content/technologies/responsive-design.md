## What you will learn

Most of the world browses the web on a phone. Responsive design is the discipline of making your pages adapt to any screen size — and it is a core expectation of every front-end job.

This stage will cover:

- Mobile-first thinking and fluid layouts
- Responsive images and typography
- Advanced media queries and breakpoints
- Modern container queries
- Designing and testing across devices
- Common responsive patterns (navbars, grids, cards, tables)
