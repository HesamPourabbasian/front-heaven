## What you will learn

Modern front-end development happens inside frameworks. Frameworks give you reusable components, reactive state, routing and tooling — the patterns that make complex applications possible.

This stage will cover:

- Why frameworks exist and what problems they solve
- The component model and props
- Reactivity and state management
- Routing and navigation in real apps
- Data fetching and side effects
- React, Vue or Svelte — and how to choose
- Framework-agnostic skills that transfer everywhere
